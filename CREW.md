# Crew of The Long Silence

Full names, backstories, and dialogue voice notes for the 5 crew, written
in step 3 to fill in what README.md's faction-conflict roles left open.
Do not contradict README.md's Crew section (faction leanings, role
descriptions) — this file only adds names, history, and voice on top of
that canon. Step 4 (branching dialogue system) and step 5 (decision

detail.

Placeholder in-game flavor lines (shown on interact in step 3) live in

## Dessa Okafor — Captain

**Faction leaning:** None declared — that's the point. Compact-born, but
spent most of her adult life renouncing what that means.

**Backstory:** Grew up in a Compact arcology on Earth, trained as a supply
logistics officer before walking out on a promising, soul-crushing career
tracking ration allocations for a system she'd stopped believing served
anyone. Bought her way onto a salvage crew in the Belt with a forged
credential and never looked back. Has owned The Long Silence for eleven
years, mostly by outliving or out-negotiating everyone who tried to take
it from her. Takes gray-market jobs from all three factions when the ship
needs it, and carries the weight of a few she's not proud of — a run that
got someone else's crew killed, a debt to a Ridgeline quartermaster she
still hasn't settled. Holds the crew together less through inspiration
and more through sheer refusal to let the ship fall apart around her.

**Dialogue voice:** Terse, dry, deflects sentiment with dark humor. Gives
orders phrased as suggestions but expects them followed. Never explains
herself unless cornered. Doesn't perform authority — she just has it.
Avoid: pep talks, moralizing, long speeches.

## Kaia Brenn — Pilot

**Faction leaning:** Ex-Ridgeline Authority (defector).

**Backstory:** Flew interceptor patrols for the Ridgeline Authority for
nine years, one of their best, until she refused an order to fire on a
Drift transport that turned out to be full of evacuated miners, not
smugglers as briefed. Court-martialed in absentia after she ran. Still
flies with the discipline of someone who expects to be graded on it —
checklists, formation-thinking, precise language — but hasn't figured out
how to want anything without a chain of command telling her it's allowed.
Trusts the Captain's flying more than her politics. Uneasy around anyone
who reminds her of Ridgeline brass.

**Dialogue voice:** Clipped, precise, uses military phrasing out of habit
("Copy that," "confirmed," rank-adjacent address even when it doesn't
apply). Guarded about feelings, restless when idle, most relaxed mid-crisis
because it's the one context she still knows the rules for. Avoid: casual
slang, uncertainty in her own competence.

## Corwin Talus — Engineer

**Faction leaning:** Drift loyalist.

**Backstory:** Born and raised on a Belt mining station, learned to keep
failing equipment alive before he learned to read. Signed onto The Long
Silence young, and the ship became the closest thing he has to home turf —
he knows every seam of it better than he knows his own family's station,
which the Compact taxed into abandonment years back. Deeply committed to
the Drift cause: cheap, safe, independent outer-system shipping that
doesn't answer to Earth or Mars. Respects the Captain's skill but is
openly wary of her willingness to cut deals with the Compact and Ridgeline
just to keep the lights on — to him, every compromise is a small betrayal
of the people the Drift is supposed to protect.

**Dialogue voice:** Blunt, technical, drops into jargon when he's
comfortable and clipped one-liners when he's not. Passionate and a little
self-righteous about the ship and the Drift; visibly suspicious of Compact
or Ridgeline errands. Warms up slowly, shows trust through information
sharing rather than warmth. Avoid: diplomatic hedging, deference to
authority for its own sake.

## Amara Voss — Medic/Quartermaster

**Faction leaning:** Compact-born refugee.

**Backstory:** Fled a Compact resource-rationing crackdown on a mid-tier
Earth city where her family's block lost its water allocation over an
administrative reclassification — nobody's fault, everybody's problem,
exactly the kind of bureaucratic violence the Compact specializes in.
Trained as a field medic before she left, now doubles as quartermaster
because someone has to track what's actually on this ship and Amara
refuses to let inventory decisions be made without a conscience attached.
Quietly political: doesn't lecture, but consistently pushes the crew
toward the choice that costs them more and helps more people. The moral
center of the crew, though she'd never claim the title.

**Dialogue voice:** Soft-spoken, deliberate word choice, asks questions
rather than making demands. Slips in moral framing without moralizing
directly — states facts about who a decision affects and lets it sit.
Warm but not soft; has seen enough scarcity to be practical about it.
Avoid: naive idealism, raised voice, ultimatums.

## Marcus Reyn — Security

**Faction leaning:** Undeclared / uncertain — intentionally the crew's
open question.

**Backstory:** Ex-something — the specifics are the point of not knowing
yet. Carries himself like former Ridgeline muscle but the timeline doesn't
quite add up, and he's been evasive enough times that Corwin's stopped
asking. Took the security job on The Long Silence for reasons he hasn't
shared, and has done it well enough that nobody's pushed hard on his
references. Loyalty to the crew currently reads as genuine day-to-day, but
he takes occasional unexplained absences at ports and receives comms he
doesn't discuss — deliberately left open as the source of mid-game
tension step 5 should resolve.

**Dialogue voice:** Clipped, guarded, redirects personal questions without
being rude about it ("ask me something I'll actually answer"). Competent
and calm under pressure, dryly polite rather than warm. Never lies
outright but is comfortable letting silence do the lying for him. Avoid:
volunteering backstory, false bravado, cruelty — his guardedness should
read as controlled, not hostile.
